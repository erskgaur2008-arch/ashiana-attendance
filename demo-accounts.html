How to access a real account

  1. Return to the login page and choose the correct role.
  2. Enter the credentials provided by the School. Sample IDs on this page cannot be used to sign in.
  3. If your account or PIN is not working, use the available recovery option or contact an authorized School administrator.
  4. Never share your password, PIN, OTP or banking credentials with anyone.
