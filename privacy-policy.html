This Privacy Policy explains how Ashiana Public School (“the School”, “we”) handles personal information through the Ashiana Attendance Application, including its website and supported mobile/PWA experiences.

1. Information we process

Depending on the account and enabled features, information may include student names, admission and roll numbers, class and section, date of birth, parent/guardian details, contact information, address, photographs, staff and employee IDs, role assignments, account identifiers, attendance records, leave requests, notices, homework, timetables, school calendar information and fee-related details.

Authentication and technical data may include session information, login identifiers, security logs, passkey public-key credentials, device/app information and push-notification tokens.

2. Attendance and location

Attendance features may process timestamps, QR-code scan results, check-in/check-out records and related verification data. Staff location-based attendance may use location coordinates or a location-verification result to check whether attendance is recorded within an authorized school area. Location permissions are controlled by the device or browser, and location information should be used only for disclosed school attendance purposes.

3. Biometrics and passkeys

Where supported, device biometrics may be used by the device operating system to confirm the user or unlock an authenticated session. Passkeys may involve cryptographic public-key credentials and authentication results. The Application is not intended to collect raw fingerprint images, facial scans or biometric templates; the exact data flow depends on the device and implementation.

4. How we use information

Information is used to administer school accounts, verify users, record attendance, manage student/staff records and leave requests, deliver school communications, provide enabled academic and fee features, maintain security, investigate errors and meet applicable legal or recordkeeping obligations.

5. Children’s information

The Application may process children’s personal information for school-related purposes. Access should be limited to authorized persons with a legitimate need. Where applicable law requires verifiable parental/guardian consent or other safeguards, the School must implement them. Parents or guardians may contact the School regarding a child’s records or privacy concerns.

6. Sharing

Information may be accessed by authorized administrators, teachers and staff according to their roles, and processed by service providers supporting hosting, databases, authentication, storage, notifications or other enabled functions. Information may also be disclosed where required or permitted by law. We do not sell student or staff personal information to advertisers.

7. Security and access

The Application may use role-based access, authentication, session controls, database permissions and secure network connections. No online system can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their credentials and devices and report suspected unauthorized access to the School.

8. Retention

Records are retained for as long as reasonably necessary for school administration, applicable retention requirements, security, dispute resolution and legal obligations. Retention periods vary by record type. Information no longer required should be securely deleted or otherwise handled as permitted by law.

9. Cookies and device storage

The Application may use browser storage, cookies or similar technologies to maintain sessions, support security and provide core features. Clearing browser data may sign you out.

10. Third-party services

External hosting, authentication, notification or payment services may process information to provide their functions. When you use an external payment service or follow an external link, that provider’s terms and privacy policy may also apply.

11. Your requests and complaints

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to or correction of personal information, seek deletion where available, withdraw consent where applicable, or raise a complaint. The School may need to verify identity or parental/guardian authority and may retain records where legally required.

12. Applicable law and updates

We will handle personal information in accordance with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and applicable rules and commencement provisions to the extent in force and applicable. This policy may be updated as features, practices or legal requirements change; the revised version will show its updated date.

13. Contact

Contact Ashiana Public School using the official email, address and telephone details published by the School. Replace the contact placeholder above before making this policy public. Do not send passwords, PINs or authentication secrets in privacy requests.
