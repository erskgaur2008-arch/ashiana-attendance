Frequently asked questions

I cannot log in or the app returns to the login screen

Confirm you selected the correct role and are using the school-issued login details. Check your internet connection, update/reopen the app, and try again. Do not share your password or PIN. If the issue continues, ask the authorised school administrator to verify your account and role assignment.

My dashboard disappears after refreshing or reopening the app

Check that the app is updated and your connection is stable. Sign in again if your session has expired. If this happens repeatedly, record your role, device/browser, and the steps that reproduce it, then contact the school administrator. Never send session tokens or passwords.

My attendance QR code is not scanning

Allow camera access, clean the camera lens, increase screen brightness, and keep the code inside the scan frame. Check that the code is current and belongs to the correct student/staff member. If it is still rejected, ask the authorised administrator to verify the record.

Staff attendance says location permission is required

Enable location services on your device and allow location access for the app/browser when prompted. Make sure you are at the authorised school location and that the device can obtain a location fix. If the location remains unavailable, contact the school administrator; do not attempt to bypass location checks.

Fingerprint, face unlock, or passkey is not working

Confirm a screen lock and a passkey provider are configured on the device. Use the app's available enable/disable control and follow the device prompt. If the device does not support the feature, use the normal school-approved sign-in method.

Where can I check a leave request?

Open the Student portal and use the Leave Request / Leave Status options. If the status is missing or incorrect, note the submission date and ask the authorised school administrator to check the request.

I cannot see fees or my payment confirmation

Check that the correct student account is open and review the fee items shown in the portal. Before paying, verify the displayed amount and UPI details. Keep your transaction reference. If a payment is pending or a receipt is missing, contact the school office/authorised administrator; do not submit repeated payments without checking.

I am not receiving notifications

Check internet access, app/browser notification permission, and Android notification settings. Open the app to refresh its data. Some notifications may be delayed by device settings or network availability; verify important notices inside the portal as well.

My student/staff details or class assignment are incorrect

For safety, profile and class corrections must be handled by an authorised school administrator. Share only the minimum details needed to identify the record, and never include passwords, PINs, or authentication codes.
