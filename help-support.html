Frequently Asked Questions

I cannot log in. What should I do?

Check that you selected the correct role and entered the correct admission number, Employee ID, email, password or PIN. Check your internet connection and reload the page. If the problem continues, contact the School office. Never send your password or PIN to support.

What if the dashboard returns to the login screen?

Check that your browser or app is up to date and that your device has a stable connection. Sign in again. If the issue repeats, report the device type, role and approximate time of the problem—do not include authentication secrets.

I forgot my student password or staff PIN.

Use the recovery or Reset PIN option provided on the relevant login screen, if available. If recovery does not work, contact the authorized School administrator to verify your identity and help restore access.

My attendance is missing or incorrect.

Note the date and the attendance entry that appears incorrect. Contact the class teacher or authorized School administrator for a review. Do not try to change attendance records through unofficial methods.

The QR code is not scanning.

Increase screen brightness, clean the camera lens, allow camera permission when requested, and hold the code steady inside the scanner frame. If it still fails, ask the authorized teacher or School office for assistance.

Staff location permission is not working.

Enable Location services on the device and allow location access for the browser or app. Ensure you are using the supported app/page and are at the authorized attendance location. If permission was denied earlier, change it in device or browser settings and retry.

Fingerprint or passkey authentication is not working.

Confirm that your device supports the offered authentication method and that screen lock/biometrics are configured on the device. Use the other authorized login or recovery method if available. Device biometrics and passkeys are not identical; available features depend on your platform.

My leave request is still pending.

Submitting a request does not automatically approve it. Check the request status and contact the responsible teacher or School office if a response is overdue.

I paid a fee but it still shows pending.

Keep your transaction receipt/reference and contact the School office with the payment date, amount and transaction reference. Never share a UPI PIN, bank password or OTP. A QR code or payment instruction alone is not proof of payment.

I am not receiving notifications.

Check device notification permissions, internet connectivity, battery/background restrictions and whether the app is allowed to send notifications. Open the Application to check for updates directly; push delivery can be delayed by device or network settings.

How do I correct my personal information or report a privacy concern?

Contact the School using its official contact channel. For a privacy request, include only enough information to identify the record safely. Do not include passwords, PINs or one-time codes.
